# Image credits & replacement guide

All photography in `/assets/images` is **placeholder stock** from Unsplash and Pexels (free licences), graded to one
firelit look with `scripts/grade.py`. **None of it shows a real Kaa**: the food, rooms, bar and people are stand-ins,
and the city at night is used for atmosphere.

**Before launch, replace them with the restaurant's own photography** (the hearth and cooks, real plates, the
roof at sunset, the bar). Keep the same filename to swap in place, then run
`python scripts/grade.py <raw-photos> assets/images` to match the grade.

The hero's coals are `embers.jpg`, brought to life by the shader in `components/EmberField.tsx`; any close-up of
glowing charcoal works as a replacement.
